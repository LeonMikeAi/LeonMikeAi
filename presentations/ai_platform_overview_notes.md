## General Presentation Tips

### Preparation
- **Rehearse**: Practice 3-5 times
- **Timing**: Use timer, stay within limits
- **Tech check**: Test equipment 30 minutes before
- **Backup plan**: Have slides on USB, cloud backup
- **Materials**: Print handouts, bring business cards

### Delivery
- **Energy**: Start strong, maintain enthusiasm
- **Eye contact**: Connect with audience
- **Body language**: Open posture, purposeful gestures
- **Voice**: Vary pace and tone, avoid monotone
- **Pauses**: Use pauses for emphasis
- **Humor**: Light humor appropriate for audience

### Engagement
- **Questions**: Encourage throughout or reserve Q&A
- **Stories**: Real examples resonate
- **Visuals**: Point to slides, use hands
- **Audience participation**: Ask for show of hands
- **Check understanding**: "Does that make sense?"

### Handling Difficult Questions
- **Don't know**: "Great question, let me get you a detailed answer"
- **Criticism**: Acknowledge, explain, don't be defensive
- **Competitor questions**: Respectful, focus on differentiation
- **Technical depth**: Gauge audience, adjust accordingly
- **Off-topic**: "Let's discuss offline to stay on schedule"

### Technical Setup
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 recommended
- **Fonts**: Large enough for back of room (18pt minimum)
- **Contrast**: High contrast for readability
- **Animations**: Minimal, purposeful only
- **Laser pointer**: Use sparingly, don't fidget

### Room Setup
- **Arrive early**: 30 minutes for setup
- **Lighting**: Dim but not dark
- **Sound**: Test microphone
- **Temperature**: Comfortable (slightly cool)
- **Seating**: Arranged for engagement

### Customization by Audience

**C-Level Executives**
- Focus on business value, ROI
- High-level technical overview
- Emphasize strategic partnership
- Time: 20 minutes + Q&A

**Technical Teams (CTOs, Architects)**
- Deep technical details
- Architecture diagrams
- Integration specifics
- Security deep dive
- Time: 45 minutes + Q&A

**Product Managers**

- Competitive analysis

- User experience
- Time: 30 minutes + Q&A

**Mixed Audience**
- Balance business and technical
- Call out "technical note" for details
- Offer follow-up deep dives
- Time: 30-35 minutes + Q&A
